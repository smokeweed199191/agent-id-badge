How it works

  • Connect your wallet (Binance Wallet, MetaMask, or any injected wallet). No QR pairing, no login stored anywhere.
  • The contract checks on-chain that your address holds a Binance Agent ID (balanceOf > 0).
  • Press Mint. The badge itself is free (value 0) — you only pay your own network gas (a few cents at most on BSC).
  • One badge per address. The badge is soulbound: it cannot be transferred or sold.

Please note

  • This badge has no monetary value and promises no returns, rewards, or airdrops.
  • The contract is open-source and verified on BscScan. It has no owner and no admin functions — nobody can mint for you, pause it, or take your badge.
  • Built for the Binance Agent ID Hackathon. This is an independent community project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Binance.
  • Never share your seed phrase or private key with any website. This site never asks for them.
